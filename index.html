03 / DATA CONTRACT

What you can — and cannot — conclude

Supported by CAA data
  • 2024 and 2025 total passengers by selected airport pair
  • Absolute and percentage passenger change
  • Relative scale and rank within this curated selection
  • Screening markets for further investigation
Not supported by CAA Table 12.1 alone
  • Any individual airline’s revenue, share or profitability
  • Actual average fares, load factors or available seats
  • Month-by-month seasonality in this annual dataset
  • Real codeshare options, connection times or QSI scores
